# ⚡ G-Warp

G-Warp is a block-based game maker. It works the way Scratch, TurboWarp and PenguinMod work, with its own dark, electric look, a mascot named G-Spark, a song maker and 191 extensions.

**Status:** Beta (the only beta for now). Some things may break.

## Try it
- Open the website and click **Create your first code**, or **Use your project** to open a .sb3 or .pmp file.

## Files
- `index.html`: the website
- `app.html`: the G-Warp Beta app (one single file)

## Rules we build by
1. **Same same, but different.** It works like Scratch, TurboWarp and PenguinMod, but with G-Warp's own look. The Scratch look is only an option in Settings.
2. **Theirs is theirs, ours is ours.** No Scratch art or logos. Every extension creator is credited and their licence is followed.
3. **Safety first.** Extensions that can read personal information always show a warning. No accounts. Kids under 13 need a parent's OK before any data is sent.

## Not official
G-Warp is an independent project. It isn't made by or connected to Scratch, the Scratch Foundation, TurboWarp or PenguinMod.
