WHAT IS ALREADY IN THE APP (v15.47.0, built by Claude, test: claude/tests/test_cosanim.js 27/27 PASS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FLIPBOOK (frame by frame, in the Costumes tab)
   - Onion skin button (bottom bar of the paint editor): the costume BEFORE shows faintly in violet,
     the one AFTER in green, BEHIND the drawing, lined up by rotation centre. Strip with
     before 0-3 / after 0-3 / strength. Vector + Bitmap. Never saved into the costume.
     Code: window.GW_FLIPBOOK {settings, setOnion, renderOnion, open, close, play, stop, state}
     Source: claude/flipbook4700.js
   - Play button (under the costume list): a small player flips through the costumes in order,
     lined up like on the stage. FPS 1-30, loop, "Costumes [from] to [to]". It starts with the
     group the selected costume belongs to (run 1..run 7) or all costumes.
2. ANIMATION TAB (bones, like Pivot Animator) - now for EVERYONE (was Developer mode only)
   - Costumes -> "Animation" button. Rig mode: add bones, attach shapes (or automatically).
     Animate mode: frames strip, drag bone ends, onion skin, In-between, Smooth x2, Play, FPS, loop.
     "Make costumes" turns every frame into a normal costume ("walk 1", "walk 2"...).
   - G-Spark comes with bones and two animations (walk, wave) the first time you open it.
   - Costumes made from an animation now FOLLOW its changes (no old frames), rename renames them.
   - Keys: <- -> step frames, Space plays.
   - Code: window.GW_ANIM {open, close, solve(anim,pose), deltas, applyPose, frameSvg(anim,i),
     partPaths, makeCostumes(sprite,anim), newAnim, undo, redo, tween, smooth, mix(A,B,t), state}
     Animation object: {id, name, costume, svg, rc, bones:[{id,name,parent,at,off,x,y,len,angle}],
     parts:{ "<path>": boneId }, frames:[{roots:{boneId:{x,y}}, angles:{boneId:deg}}], fps, loop, rev}
   - window.GW_MASCOT {svg, bones(), walkFrames(), waveFrames()}  (G-Spark's own drawing + rig)
3. ANIMATION BLOCKS (palette section "Animation", shows when the sprite has an animation)
   switch to animation [ ] / play animation [ ] from frame [ ] / play animation [ ] over and over /
   stop animation.  Code: window.GW_ANIM_BLOCKS {start, stop, frameIdx, ensureFrames, refresh}
