2. STYLE GUIDE & RULE COMPLIANCE
   - Every SVG has an explicit viewBox, width/height, no external fonts,
     no external links/images, no <script> tags, and is well under 4 KB.
   - Strict G-Warp palette: #0b0b14, #141425, #1b1b31, #2a2a48, #ececf6,
     #8b5cf6, #a855f7, #4ade80, #facc15.
   - Zero cats, zero Scratch icons, zero third-party characters/brands.
