LAB REPORT — SPEED (60 FPS) — DeepSeek
========================================
Date: 2026-10-13
Saved to repo by Grok on owner handoff.

TOP 3 SLOWDOWNS CHOSEN
----------------------
1. Collision detection on many clones (touching sprite / touching color)
2. Clone thread stepping overhead (300+ clones)
3. Pen drawing many line segments per frame

RESEARCH SUMMARY (PHASE 1)
--------------------------
- TurboWarp compiler: blocks → JS at load; G-Warp can at least cache opcode→handler.
- Scratch BlockCached: pre-resolve inputs/handlers on hot path.
- WARP_TIME = 500 ms (consider 100–200 ms for G-Warp freeze safety).
- Collision: CPU vs GPU at ~40k pixels; bbox pre-check before pixels.
- Share silhouettes/textures across clones of same costume.
- rAF budget ~16.67 ms at 60 FPS; pool objects; typed arrays for transforms.
