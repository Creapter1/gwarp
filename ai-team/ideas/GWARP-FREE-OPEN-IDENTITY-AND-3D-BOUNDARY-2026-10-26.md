# G-Warp: Free Creative Core, Open Reuse, and a Separate Far-Future 3D Product

**Date:** 2026-10-26  
**Owner intent:** recorded from the owner's message on 2026-10-26.  
**Status:** Strategy / future idea only. No implementation, pricing, licensing or release decision.

## 1. The goal is not competition for its own sake

G-Warp's ambition has two valid success paths:

1. **Capability success:** if the team can eventually make G-Warp better than TurboWarp and PenguinMod in meaningful areas, pursue that honestly with evidence.
2. **Recognition success:** if it cannot surpass those platforms overall, become known and respected for something distinctive that creators genuinely value.

These are not competing goals. G-Warp should keep striving for exceptional quality without turning the project into a race to copy every feature or win a popularity contest.

The strongest current identity candidates remain:
- Music creation and composition inside a block-coding environment.
- Character animation, rigging and reusable animation assets.
- A joined-up workflow: make a song, animate a character, and use both in an interactive project.
- Project-aware AI as a possible later advantage, only after it becomes useful, reliable, safe and privacy-transparent.

**Beta honesty:** current G-Warp features are still beta. These are product directions, not claims that the music studio, animation studio, compatibility or AI are finished or already best-in-class.

## 2. Keep the G-Warp core and creative resources free and open

The owner's intent is that G-Warp's core and reusable resources remain freely available. The project should welcome other Scratch-derived communities and creators reusing ideas, learning from them, adapting them, and building their own versions.

Principles:
- Do not create artificial paywalls around the current core editor or the creative ideas documented for it.
- Keep the current G-Warp repository licence (MIT) unless the owner explicitly chooses otherwise after reviewing the consequences.
- Make original G-Warp resources reusable under the licence that actually applies to each resource.
- Keep creator attribution and third-party licence obligations clear. A resource is not automatically free of restrictions merely because it is linked from G-Warp.
- Do not imply that TurboWarp, PenguinMod or Scratch endorse G-Warp or any reuse.
- Treat community contributions as welcome. Any future extension or asset gallery should make its licence/permissions visible so users know what they may reuse.
- The Plus extension idea should follow the existing safe, bounded API plan; it must not grant add-ons unrestricted access to project data, the network, or arbitrary JavaScript execution.

The owner explicitly welcomes other platforms adopting useful G-Warp ideas. This is a positive open-ecosystem principle, not a requirement to surrender attribution or ignore licences.

## 3. Keep the future 3D idea separate from the free 2D core

The existing ai-team/ideas/PLUS-EXTENSIONS-AND-3D-2026-10-26.md describes a far-future G-Warp 3D direction:
- A simple browser-based 3D runner.
- A fuller desktop version with the same familiar block-coding idea, but 3D sprites/world/stage and additional capabilities.

This is interpreted as the separate product the owner says may eventually contain enough extra value to justify a price. That interpretation is based on the existing 3D idea document; the owner has not selected a final business model.

Possible future boundary:
- **G-Warp core (2D):** remains free and open.
- **Community resources / Plus extensions:** intended to be shareable and reusable, with clear creator licences; do not assume a paid marketplace.
- **G-Warp 3D / expanded desktop experience:** may be evaluated as a separate product or edition if it delivers substantial additional capabilities and ongoing value. A price is only a possibility, not a decision.

Do not:
- Charge for the existing G-Warp core merely to fund the 3D experiment.
- Make current users pay to keep using projects, music, animation, or assets they already created.
- Promise that all future 3D features will be paid or free before the owner decides.
- Copy Scratch/TurboWarp/PenguinMod branding, art, code or proprietary assets into the separate product.
- Change the current MIT licence, add a paywall, introduce accounts, or build payment infrastructure as part of this idea.

## 4. Licensing and product-boundary review required before any paid edition

The current repository's LICENSE is MIT. That licence permits commercial use and redistribution subject to its terms; a future paid product cannot silently take away rights already granted to users of MIT-licensed code.

Before any separate paid product is built or sold, review:
1. Which code remains MIT/open source and which genuinely new components, if any, are separate.
2. Contributor rights and any third-party library, extension, model, font, music, sound or art licences.
3. Whether the separate product can be distributed without violating the licences of the free core or its dependencies.
4. How project files and assets move between free G-Warp and the 3D product.
5. What the paid product adds that is substantial, clearly explained, and not a paywall around existing free work.

No legal conclusion or licence change is made by this idea note. Obtain appropriate legal/licensing advice before commercial release.
