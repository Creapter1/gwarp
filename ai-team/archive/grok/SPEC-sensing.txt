G-WARP SPEC: SENSING (Scratch 3.0 behaviour)
Source: scratchfoundation/scratch-vm src/blocks/scratch3_sensing.js + util/cast.js
Written by: Grok  |  Date: 2026-10-13

=====================================================================
TOUCH / DISTANCE / COLOUR
=====================================================================
--- sensing_touchingobject ---
util.target.isTouchingObject(menu). Menu includes sprites, _mouse_, _edge_.

--- sensing_touchingcolor ---
Color via Cast.toRgbColorList (hex #rrggbb or decimal packed RGB).

--- sensing_coloristouchingcolor ---
Two colours: mask and target. colorIsTouchingColor on the drawable.

--- sensing_distanceto ---
Stage target always reports 10000.
_mouse_ or sprite name; missing sprite -> 10000.
Otherwise Euclidean distance from this sprite's x/y.

=====================================================================
MOUSE / KEYS / DRAG
=====================================================================
--- sensing_mousex / sensing_mousey / sensing_mousedown ---
IO queries on mouse (Scratch coords).

--- sensing_keypressed ---
keyboard.getKeyIsDown(KEY_OPTION). Includes "any".

--- sensing_setdragmode ---
Draggable iff DRAG_MODE === "draggable".

=====================================================================
TIMER / LOUDNESS / TIME
=====================================================================
--- sensing_timer / sensing_resettimer ---
projectTimer / resetProjectTimer via clock IO.

--- sensing_loudness ---
-1 if no audioEngine or currentStepTime null.
Cached once per step (currentStepTime window).

--- sensing_loud ---
loudness > 10.

--- sensing_current ---
Menu year, month (1-12), date, dayofweek (Sun=1), hour, minute, second.
Local timezone Date.

--- sensing_dayssince2000 ---
Fractional days since 2000-01-01 local, with DST offset adjustment.

=====================================================================
ASK / ANSWER / USER
=====================================================================
--- sensing_askandwait ---
Queues question. If target visible and not stage, shows say bubble with
the question text and emits empty QUESTION prompt; else emits QUESTION
with the text. Returns a Promise resolved on ANSWER event.
Stop all / stop target clears pending questions.

--- sensing_answer ---
Last answer string (reset on project start / runtime dispose).

--- sensing_username ---
userData.getUsername (empty string when not logged in).

--- sensing_userid ---
Legacy no-op.

=====================================================================
sensing_of
=====================================================================
OBJECT: _stage_ or sprite name. Missing target -> 0.
Stage properties: backdrop #, backdrop name, volume, background # (1.4 alias).
Sprite: x position, y position, direction, costume #, costume name, size, volume.
Else treat PROPERTY as variable name on that target; missing -> 0.
Costume/backdrop numbers are 1-based (currentCostume + 1).

=====================================================================
EDGE CASES
=====================================================================
- distance to missing sprite = 10000 (not 0).
- ask queue is FIFO; only one prompt active.
- loudness measured once per frame step.
- colour args: invalid hex falls back toward black via Cast colour path.

=====================================================================
TURBOWARP / PENGUINMOD
=====================================================================
- Same sensing semantics for compatibility.
- Microphone permission may differ by browser; -1 when unavailable is correct.
