G-WARP SPEC: SOUND (Scratch 3.0 behaviour)
Source: scratchfoundation/scratch-vm src/blocks/scratch3_sound.js
Written by: Grok  |  Date: 2026-10-13

=====================================================================
RANGES
=====================================================================
Volume: clamp 0..100
Pitch effect: -360..360 (~ -3 to +3 octaves)
Pan effect: -100..100
MIDI note (music ext, related): 36..96
Beat duration clamp (music): 0..100
Tempo (music): 20..500 bpm

Green flag / stop all: clear effects for all targets; stop all sounds.
Stop for target: stop only that target's waiting play-until-done sounds.

=====================================================================
BLOCKS
=====================================================================
--- sound_play ---
Play sound by menu name or 1-based index (wrapClamp into list). Does not wait.
Missing sound / empty list: no-op.

--- sound_playuntildone ---
Same play, stores soundId as waiting; returns promise from soundBank.
Stop target clears waiting set and stops those sounds.

--- sound_stopallsounds ---
Stop every target's soundBank sounds; clear waiting sets.

--- sound_seteffectto / sound_changeeffectby ---
EFFECT pitch|pan (case-insensitive). set assigns; change adds.
Then clamp to EFFECT_RANGE. Yields a tick (Promise.resolve).
Unknown effect name: no-op.

--- sound_cleareffects ---
All effects on this target -> 0; sync soundBank.

--- sound_setvolumeto / sound_changevolumeby / sound_volume ---
Volume 0..100 on target; change adds then clamp. Reporter returns volume.
Yields a tick after set/change.

--- sound_sounds_menu / beats_menu / effects_menu ---
Pass-through menu reporters.

=====================================================================
SOUND INDEX RULES
=====================================================================
1) Match by exact sound name.
2) Else parseInt as 1-based index, wrapClamp into 0..len-1.
3) Else -1 (no play).

Clones inherit a copy of sound state from the source target.

=====================================================================
TURBOWARP / PENGUINMOD
=====================================================================
Same semantics. Audio engine backend may differ; results for volume/effects
should match when hardware allows.

=====================================================================
G-WARP NOTES
=====================================================================
- play until done must cooperate with Stop (clear waiting sounds).
- Do not leak AudioBufferSource nodes across stop all.
