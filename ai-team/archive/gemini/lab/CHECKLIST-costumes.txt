KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS (from Scratch)
---------------------------------
S = Select, A = Reshape, B = Brush, E = Eraser, F = Fill,
T = Text, L = Line, C = Circle.
Ctrl+Z = Undo, Ctrl+Y = Redo, Ctrl+C/V = Copy/Paste,
Delete = Remove selection, Shift = constrain shapes.
