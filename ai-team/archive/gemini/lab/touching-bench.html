Why Grid of Cells Wins

With 1,000 moving boxes, checking every pair requires 1000 × 999 / 2 = 499,500 AABB overlap tests every single frame.

Partitioning the stage into a Grid of Cells buckets each box into the cells it overlaps and only tests boxes sharing a cell, cutting pair checks by ~40×–80× with identical collision results.
