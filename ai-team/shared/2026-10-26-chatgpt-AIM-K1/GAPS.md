# AIM-K1 — Recipe gaps

- **Motion:** `direction` has no recipe example yet.
- **Looks:** no recipe example for `think_secs`, `go_front_back`, `go_layers`, `costume_number`, `costume_name`, or `size_reporter`.
- **Sound:** no recipe example for `snd_change_effect`, `snd_clear_effects`, `change_volume`, or `volume_reporter`.
- **Events:** no recipe example for `when_backdrop`.
- **Control:** no block-type gap in the current catalog; all 10 Control types appear in at least one existing recipe.
- **Sensing:** no recipe example for `touching_pointer`, `key_pressed`, `mouse_down`, `mouse_y`, `touching_sprite`, `distance_to`, `username`, `current_time`, or `days_since_2000`.
- **Operators:** no recipe example for `op_add`, `op_gt`, `op_lt`, `op_eq`, `op_and`, `op_or`, `op_not`, `op_mod`, `op_abs`, `op_mathop`, or `op_contains`.
- **Stage:** no recipe example for `backdrop_number`, `backdrop_name`, or `change_backdrop_color`.
- **Lists:** no recipe example for `list_delete`, `list_insert`, `list_replace`, `list_hide`, `list_contents`, or `list_contains`.
- **My Blocks:** no recipe example for `define_custom` or `call_custom`.
- **Variables:** no recipe example for `hide_var`.
- **Tilemap:** no recipe examples for `tile_set`, `tile_at`, `tile_is_solid`, `tile_clear_layer`, or `touching_tile`.
- **Behaviors:** no recipe examples for `behavior_get_attr`, `behavior_set_attr`, `behavior_change_attr`, `behavior_event_actor_name`, or `behavior_event_tile`.
- **G-Warp extensions:** no recipe examples yet for the remaining camera, layer, runtime-size/FPS, one-sound-loop, key-simulation, and physics controls: `cam_set_y`, `cam_change_x`, `cam_change_y`, `cam_set_zoom`, `cam_change_zoom`, `cam_move`, `cam_set_dir`, `cam_turn_cw`, `cam_turn_ccw`, `ext_set_layer`, `rt_set_fps`, `rt_set_size`, `snd_stop_one`, `snd_loop`, `key_sim`, `phys_set_gravity`, `phys_disable`, `phys_set_tickrate`, `phys_set_pos`, `phys_set_vel`, `phys_change_vel`, `phys_push`, `phys_spin`, `phys_set_spin`, `phys_set_fixed`, `phys_density`, `phys_density_n`, `phys_friction`, `phys_friction_n`, `phys_bounce_n`, `phys_props`, `phys_set_scroll`, `phys_change_scroll`, `cam_set_col`, and `angle_costume`.
