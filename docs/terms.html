To count browsers once, it uses a small marker in browser local storage; to show “online now,” it sends a random, temporary session code. The dashboard displays totals, not names or exact locations, and the counter is not used for ads or cross-site advertising. Firebase and the network providers that carry requests may process technical data such as an IP address under their own policies. See Safety and privacy and Firebase privacy information.

Some desktop builds create diagnostic reports on your device. Read a report before sharing it: it may contain project details or a record of actions needed to reproduce a bug.
