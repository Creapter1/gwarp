Tutorial 1: Make G-Spark walk

You will make: a short costume animation that repeats while the project runs. You will use Flipbook/Onion skin and four blocks: when flag clicked, forever, next costume and wait (0.1) seconds.

Part A — Make the walking poses

  1. Open G-Warp and select G-Spark in the sprite list. Open Costumes.
  2. Keep the original costume as a backup. Duplicate it to make a second costume, then change the arm or leg angle a little. Duplicate again for a third pose. Make each pose only slightly different so the movement looks smooth.
  3. Rename the copies in a clear order such as walk 1, walk 2, and walk 3. Check that they appear in that order in the costume list.
  4. Open the paint editor for a pose and click Onion skin in the bottom bar. The previous/next costume can appear faintly behind your drawing as a guide. The faint guide is an overlay; it should not be saved into the costume.
  5. Use the Play control below the costume list to preview the costumes flipping in order. If the feet or arms jump too far, edit the pose and preview again.

Part B — Make it repeat with blocks

  1. Open Code and choose Events. Drag when flag clicked into an empty part of the workspace.
  2. Choose Control and snap forever underneath the hat block.
  3. Inside forever, add next costume from Looks, then add wait (0.1) seconds from Control under it.
when flag clicked
forever
  next costume
  wait (0.1) seconds

Test it

  1. Click the Green Flag/Start control. G-Spark should keep changing costumes.
  2. Change the wait number: a smaller delay looks faster, a larger delay looks slower. Press Stop when you are done.
  3. Save the project, switch away from Costumes and back, then reopen the saved copy to make sure the poses remain.

Gotcha: next costume follows the costume-list order and cycles around. If the walk looks backward or jumps, reorder or redraw the poses before changing more blocks.
